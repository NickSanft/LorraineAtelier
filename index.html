✦ Welcome to the Atelier

For one who does not yet know — a mono-shirazu. Here, magic is not spoken but drawn.

Press Ctrl + Alt + W to take up the pen and inscribe a sigil — △ fire, ▽ water, ○ wind, □ earth — then close the ring to seal it and a spell takes hold. A small ink companion roams your desktop, and the dock along the bottom is yours to click (try the ❧ for your Tower of Tomes). Toggle ☾ Day / Night anytime.

Welcome — your desk is now an atelier.
